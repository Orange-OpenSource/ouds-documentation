| 8 | Bottom actions | Holds the leading and trailing actions, including the send action. | N |

| 10 | Helper text | Gives additional information about the input, such as the remaining characters. | Y |
| 11 | Helper link | Links to additional help when the helper text is not sufficient. | Y |
