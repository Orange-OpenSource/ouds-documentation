| # | Element | Purpose | Optional |
|---|---------|---------|----------|
| 1 | Container | The card itself: the surface that holds the background, the bottom border and the slot. | N |
| 2 | Background | Layer that sits over the card surface and supports the visual separation of the card. | Y |
| 3 | Bottom border | Predefined line along the bottom edge of a Filled card. | Y |
| 4 | Slot | Holds the customizable content of the card, including independent interactive elements. | N |
| 4 | Skeleton | Placeholder shown while the content of the card has not loaded yet. | Y |
